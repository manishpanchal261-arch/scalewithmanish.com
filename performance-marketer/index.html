Why Hire a Performance Marketer?

Performance marketing focuses on measurable business outcomes such as qualified leads, purchases, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend. Campaign decisions are based on data instead of assumptions.

Who This Service Is For

This service is suitable for ecommerce brands, local businesses, startups, consultants and companies looking to scale customer acquisition through paid advertising.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a performance marketer do?

A performance marketer plans, manages and optimizes paid campaigns with a focus on measurable results such as leads, purchases and revenue.

Which platforms do you manage?

Campaign management can include Google Ads, Meta Ads, Search, Shopping, Performance Max and remarketing.

Can you audit existing campaigns?

Yes. Existing campaigns can be reviewed for tracking, targeting, creatives, bidding, landing pages and wasted advertising spend.
