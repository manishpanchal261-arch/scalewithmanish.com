What Does a Performance Marketer Actually Do?

A performance marketer connects advertising activity with measurable business results rather than judging success only by clicks, impressions or platform dashboards.

Customer Acquisition

Evaluate how efficiently advertising turns budget into qualified leads, customers and revenue.

Testing

Test audiences, keywords, offers, messaging, creative angles and landing-page opportunities.

Optimisation

Use conversion data, CAC, CPA, ROAS and lead quality to decide what should be improved, stopped or scaled.

Performance Marketing Goes Beyond ROAS

Platform ROAS is useful, but it does not always tell the complete profitability story. Campaign decisions should also consider the economics behind every acquisition.

A campaign can report strong ROAS while still creating weak margins, low-quality customers or an unsustainable acquisition cost.

CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost
CPA — Cost Per Acquisition
ROAS — Return on Ad Spend
AOV — Average Order Value
Conversion Rate — Traffic to Customer Efficiency
Lead Quality — Whether conversions create real business value

Frequently Asked Questions About Performance Marketing

Answers to common questions about performance marketers, paid acquisition, campaign metrics and advertising strategy.

What does a performance marketer do?

A performance marketer manages paid customer acquisition across channels such as Google Ads and Meta Ads while using conversion tracking, CAC, ROAS, creative performance and funnel data to improve measurable business outcomes.

When should I hire a performance marketer?

Consider hiring one when advertising spend is increasing without consistent growth, tracking is unreliable, customer acquisition costs are rising or you need a clearer testing and scaling strategy.

Is performance marketing only about ROAS?

No. ROAS is useful but should be considered alongside CAC, AOV, margins, conversion rate, customer quality and overall business profitability.

Can a performance marketer manage Google Ads and Meta Ads together?

Yes. Google Ads can capture existing search demand while Meta Ads can help create and expand demand through audiences and creative testing. The right channel mix depends on the business.

Why is conversion tracking important in performance marketing?

Conversion tracking helps determine which campaigns, advertisements and channels are contributing to meaningful business outcomes. Without reliable tracking, optimisation decisions can be based on incomplete information.

Can a performance marketer audit existing campaigns?

Yes. Existing campaigns can be reviewed for conversion tracking, targeting, creative performance, search terms, bidding, landing pages, attribution and wasted advertising spend.
