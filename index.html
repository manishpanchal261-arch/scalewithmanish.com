Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads

Practical answers to common questions about hiring a Google Ads expert, account management, audits, tracking and campaign performance.

How do I hire a good Google Ads expert in India?

Look beyond reviews and certifications. Ask the Google Ads expert to explain how they approach conversion tracking, campaign structure, CPA, ROAS and optimisation. A Google Ads audit can also help you evaluate their approach before committing to ongoing management.

What should I check before hiring a Google Ads specialist?

Check the scope of work, deliverables, reporting process, KPIs and account access requirements. A Google Ads specialist should work within your account while your business retains ownership and control of its Google Ads and analytics assets.

What questions should I ask a Google Ads expert?

Ask what they would review first, how they measure conversions, which KPIs they prioritise and what they do when CPA increases or campaign performance declines. A Google Ads consultant should be able to explain the reasoning behind these decisions clearly.

Should I give a Google Ads freelancer admin access?

Not automatically. Give the level of access required to manage the campaigns while keeping ownership and primary control of the advertising account with your business.

Who should own my Google Ads account?

Your business should own and control the Google Ads account. This protects your campaign history, conversion data and advertising assets if you later change freelancers, consultants or agencies.

Should I get a Google Ads audit before hiring someone?

A Google Ads audit can be a useful first step. It can uncover issues with conversion tracking, campaign structure, search terms, bidding and wasted spend before you commit to ongoing management.

How do I know if a Google Ads expert is actually good?

A strong specialist should be able to explain why campaigns perform well or poorly, not just show screenshots. Look for clear thinking around tracking, search intent, CPA, ROAS, lead quality and profitability.

Which KPIs should a Google Ads expert monitor?

Common KPIs include conversions, CPA, ROAS, conversion rate and lead quality. A Google Ads consultant should choose metrics based on your business model, campaign objective and profitability targets.

How much does Google Ads management cost in India?

Google Ads management pricing varies based on campaign complexity, advertising spend, tracking requirements and the level of strategic involvement required. The right fee should be evaluated against the scope of work rather than price alone.
