When Does Hiring a Google Ads Expert Make Sense?

An account may be generating traffic without enough useful leads or purchases. The first job is to diagnose where performance is being lost, rather than immediately increasing spend or rebuilding every campaign.

Plenty of clicks, few qualified leads

Review search intent, query relevance, lead forms, landing pages and the quality of the conversion actions used for bidding.

Sales reported, but ROAS is unclear

Check purchase values, attribution, conversion duplication and whether reporting aligns with actual order data.

Performance Max feels opaque

Review campaign goals, asset quality, feed health, audience signals, product economics and available insights.

Spend is growing faster than results

Evaluate budget allocation, marginal performance, search terms, bidding constraints and where tests are most justified.

My Approach: Diagnose Before Scaling

A repeatable evaluation process helps separate genuine performance opportunities from unreliable tracking or short-term fluctuations.

  1. Confirm business goals and conversions. Clarify what counts as a qualified enquiry or profitable sale and check how those events are measured.
  2. Inspect traffic quality. Review search terms, location settings, audience intent and the connection between ads and landing pages.
  3. Evaluate account economics. Compare CPA, ROAS, conversion quality, volume and profitability, accounting for the available data.
  4. Prioritise tests. Recommend specific changes to budgets, bidding, keywords, creative assets or tracking, with clear reasons for each.
  5. Measure and iterate. Monitor outcomes after changes and adjust based on meaningful data rather than frequent reactive edits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Google Ads expert do?

A Google Ads expert plans, audits and optimises campaigns, including search terms, bidding, budgets, conversion tracking and landing-page alignment. The goal is to improve qualified leads or sales, not simply traffic.

How much does a Google Ads expert charge in India?

Fees depend on the scope of work, monthly ad spend, number of campaigns and tracking complexity. Request a review of your account for a scope-specific quote; advertising spend is separate from management fees.

Do I need a new Google Ads account to improve performance?

Not necessarily. An existing account can often be improved through measurement fixes, search-term reviews, restructuring and testing. A new account is not a substitute for fixing strategy or tracking problems.

Can you help with ecommerce and lead generation?

Yes. The work described here covers Shopping, Performance Max and Search for ecommerce, and intent-focused Search campaigns for lead generation. The right mix depends on your product, market and data quality.

What is included in a Google Ads audit?

An initial review can cover conversion actions, keyword and search-term quality, bidding, budget distribution, campaign structure and landing-page relevance. Recommendations depend on the access and information available.

How long does Google Ads optimisation take?

Initial findings may appear quickly, but results depend on conversion volume, budgets, seasonality and the changes required. No specific ranking, lead volume or return can be guaranteed.
