Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads Experts

Answers to common questions about hiring a Google Ads expert, pricing, campaign management, audits and performance.

What does a Google Ads expert actually do?

A Google Ads expert reviews campaign structure, keywords, search terms, bidding, conversion tracking and performance data to improve lead quality, CPA, ROAS and overall advertising efficiency.

How much does a Google Ads expert charge in India?

Pricing depends on advertising spend, account complexity, campaign scope and the level of ongoing management required. The right fee should be evaluated against the work involved and the value of improved performance.

Should I hire a Google Ads freelancer or an agency?

A freelancer can be suitable when you mainly need focused Google Ads management. An agency may be more appropriate when you also need creative, landing pages, analytics or multiple advertising channels.

Should I hire a Google Ads expert or manage campaigns myself?

Managing campaigns yourself can work when the account is small and you have time to learn the platform. An expert can help when tracking, campaign complexity, wasted spend or scaling becomes difficult to manage.

Can a Google Ads expert fix an existing campaign?

Yes. Existing campaigns can be reviewed for conversion tracking, targeting, search terms, bidding, campaign structure and wasted spend before deciding whether optimisation or restructuring is required.

What should be included in Google Ads management?

Google Ads management can include campaign monitoring, search-term reviews, keyword optimisation, bidding adjustments, conversion tracking checks, reporting and recommendations based on CPA, ROAS and lead quality.
