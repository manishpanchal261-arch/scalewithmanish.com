Marketing Philosophy

Performance First, Not Vanity Metrics

Campaign decisions are evaluated around qualified leads, purchases, acquisition cost, conversion rate, revenue and return on advertising spend rather than clicks or impressions alone.

Reliable tracking, commercial search intent and controlled optimisation are important parts of the process because advertising performance cannot be evaluated accurately without dependable conversion data.
